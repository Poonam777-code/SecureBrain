Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers trick users into revealing sensitive information through fake emails or websites.

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems or steal data.

Public WiFi networks can expose your data to attackers if not encrypted.

Always check HTTPS and verify the domain before entering passwords.

Strong passwords and two-factor authentication help protect your accounts.

Suspicious popups asking for login credentials may be phishing attempts.

Downloading software from unknown websites may introduce malware.

Keeping your system updated helps fix security vulnerabilities.
